## About description (portal "About description" field)

Junk Silver Melt Calculator is a free Reddit app from Agate Precious Metals. Enter how many of each old US silver coin you have — 90% dimes, quarters and half dollars, Morgan & Peace dollars, 40% Kennedy halves, 35% War Nickels — and it tells you the total face value, the pure silver weight, and the melt value at the live silver price. No sign-up, no payment, just an answer. Values are estimates, not quotes and not financial advice.

## Featuring description (portal "Featuring description" field)

## Publish command (run from the project directory)

```sh
npx devvit publish --public
```

This submits the app for Reddit review and lists it in the public App Directory once approved. Review typically takes 1–2 business days; Reddit pauses reviews during holiday periods (Thanksgiving week may delay it).
